# Tank Warz Promo — Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

An explosive arcade battlefield cut from the supplied Tank Warz key art: bright Rustfall sky and pixel-detailed armor collide with smoke-black tactical overlays, oversized compressed type, sharp HUD rules, and forceful team-color accents. Frames should feel like a game trailer assembled from steel plates, targeting telemetry, muzzle flashes, and impact shocks. The energy is fast, confident, and slightly chaotic, while every feature callout remains readable in under two seconds.

## Colors

- `#101820` — smoke-black canvas and heavy ink
- `#F5F1D6` — steel-ivory primary text
- `#D9FF00` — acid-yellow logo/CTA energy
- `#20A8FF` — blue-team and Jackal speed accent
- `#FF7043` — orange-team, Longshot, and impact accent

## Typography

- `Barlow Condensed` — 900-weight display headlines and large mode numbers
- `IBM Plex Mono` — 300/600-weight tactical labels, specs, and supporting copy

## Motion Rules

- Use zoom-through transitions as the primary scene handoff; reserve an overexposure burn for the final hero reveal.
- Entrances should strike from different axes with short overshoot, snap, recoil, and staggered tactical readouts.
- Each key-art crop gets one restrained ambient move: lateral drift, slow push, or subtle rotation.
- Keep outgoing scenes intact until transitions take over; only the final scene may resolve to black.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not introduce purple gradients, rounded SaaS cards, glassmorphism, or clean corporate spacing.
- Do not use generic blue-on-black tech styling without the acid-yellow and orange battlefield accents.
- Do not replace the supplied key art with unrelated photoreal tanks or military stock imagery.
- Do not use soft, floaty, or uniformly eased entrances; the trailer needs weight and impact.
- Do not obscure the featured tank silhouettes or the Tank Warz title in the supplied artwork.
